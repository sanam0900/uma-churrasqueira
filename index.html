Our Story

Where Fire Meets Flavour

Uma Churrasqueira brings together the bold smoky traditions of Portuguese grilling and the vibrant spiced heritage of Nepali cuisine. Every dish is cooked over open flame — honest, rich, and full of character.

From our signature whole grilled chicken to fragrant Nepali momo and coconut prawn curry, every plate tells a story of two cultures united by a love of good food and cold beer.
